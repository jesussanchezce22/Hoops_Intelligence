How to Play / Good to Know

The essentials for your first Head-to-Head Points season.

01 · THE BASICS

How H2H Points works

Each week, your active players accumulate fantasy points based on a league-specific formula. Your total is compared with an opponent's total.

02 · DRAFT DAY

What is a snake draft?

Draft order reverses every round. If you pick last in one round, you pick first in the next.

03 · ROSTER MOVES

Waivers and free agents

Unrostered players may be added, subject to your platform's waiver rules, transaction limits, and available roster spots.

04 · TRADES

Look beyond star power

Compare fantasy output, availability, game volume, and the player you would need to drop in a multi-player trade.

05 · WEEKLY STRATEGY

Schedule advantage

More games can mean more opportunities to score. Check the actual NBA schedule and your league's lineup rules.

06 · RISK

Minutes and injuries

Role changes, playing time, and injuries can affect future value. Past performance alone cannot predict them.

Data & Settings

Import your own verified statistics. Nothing is uploaded to a server: CSV processing happens in your browser.

Import player statistics

CSV required columns: name,team,pos,pts,reb,ast,stl,blk,tov. Values are per-game averages. Optional: gp,games_next_week,source,season,as_of. GP is games played in the dataset. Games next week is a user-supplied estimate, not a live schedule. Only use data you are permitted to redistribute.

Fantasy scoring model

Illustrative default model; replace with the exact scoring rules from your league once created.

Data transparency

Demo dataset — illustrative numbers only.

This static MVP has no ESPN integration, injury feed, live NBA API, user accounts, or automatic updates. Weekly estimates multiply FPPG by user-provided upcoming games and do not predict performance. To publish fresh data, replace the bundled dataset or add an authorized scheduled data pipeline.
